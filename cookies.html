    How we use cookies and similar technologies on Home Through Form.

    Last updated: 1 January 2025

    At Home Through Form ("we", "us", or "our"), we use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website, analyse how our content is used, and support our editorial operations. This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and the choices you have.

    In short: We use a minimal set of cookies to keep the site running smoothly and to understand which articles resonate with our readers. We do not sell your data or use cookies for intrusive advertising. You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings.

    1. What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, tablet, or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners.

    Cookies may be "persistent" (remaining on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (which are deleted when you close your browser). They can also be "first-party" (set by us) or "third-party" (set by another domain, such as an analytics provider).

    2. How We Use Cookies

    We use cookies for the following purposes:

    2.1 Essential Cookies

    These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility. You may disable these by changing your browser settings, but this may affect how the website functions.

    2.2 Analytics Cookies

    We use analytics cookies to collect information about how visitors use our site — for example, which pages are most popular, how long readers stay, and how they arrive at our site. This helps us improve our editorial content and user experience. We use privacy-focused analytics where possible.

    2.3 Functionality Cookies

    These cookies allow the website to remember choices you make (such as your language preference or the region you are in) and provide enhanced, more personal features. They may also be used to remember changes you have made to text size, fonts, and other customizable elements of the site.

    2.4 Third-Party Cookies

    In some cases, we may embed content from third-party services (such as maps or social media feeds). These services may set their own cookies, which we do not control. We recommend checking the relevant third-party privacy policies for more information.

    3. Specific Cookies We Use

    The table below lists the main cookies we use and their purposes. Please note that this list may be updated from time to time as our site evolves.

    Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
    session_id Essential Maintains your session while you browse the site. Session
    cookie_consent Essential Stores your cookie preferences so we don't ask again. 1 year
    _ga Analytics Used to distinguish users for Google Analytics (if enabled). 2 years
    _gid Analytics Used to distinguish users for Google Analytics (if enabled). 24 hours
    pref_theme Functionality Remembers your display preferences (e.g., light/dark mode). 1 year

    4. Your Choices

    You have several options for managing cookies:

    • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to refuse or accept cookies, delete specific cookies, or block cookies from certain sites. The method varies by browser — check your browser's "Help" section for details.
    • Opt-out tools: For analytics cookies, you may be able to opt out using browser add-ons or industry opt-out tools. For example, Google Analytics offers a browser opt-out add-on.
    • Do Not Track: Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" signal. We do not currently respond to these signals, but we are monitoring developments in this area.

    Please note that if you disable cookies, some parts of the website may not function properly, and your experience may be affected.

    5. Changes to This Policy

    We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

    If you have any questions about our use of cookies or this policy, please contact us:
