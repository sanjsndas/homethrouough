    Our Mission

    We believe that good design writing should be useful, honest, and a pleasure to read. Too much of the conversation around interiors is either superficial trend-chasing or impenetrable theory. Home Through Form occupies a different space: thoughtful, accessible, and grounded in the realities of building and living in a home.

    Every article we publish is researched, written, and edited by people who have spent time in the field—architects, designers, and writers who care about the details that make a room feel right. We don't chase clicks, and we don't publish press releases. We write for the reader who wants to understand not just what looks good, but why it works.

    What We Stand For

    Three principles guide everything we publish.

    ◈

    Independence

    We are reader-supported and ad-free. No sponsored articles, no affiliate links, no hidden agendas. Our only obligation is to our readers.

    ◈

    Depth

    We take the time to explore subjects properly. Our articles are long-form by design, because the best design writing rewards a slower read.

    ◈

    Craft

    We care about materials, joinery, light, and proportion. Our writing reflects a deep respect for the craft of building and the people who do it.

    Our Editorial Approach

    We are often asked how we choose what to write about. The answer is simple: we look for subjects that are useful, enduring, and grounded in real-world practice.

    • No trend-chasing. We don't write about fads or flavours of the month. If a subject won't be relevant in five years, we probably won't cover it.
    • No sponsored content. We have never accepted payment for coverage, and we never will. Our recommendations are our own.
    • Primary research. Where possible, we speak to architects, makers, and manufacturers directly. We visit buildings and handle materials.
    • Corrections and transparency. If we get something wrong, we correct it openly. We believe trust is earned through honesty, not authority.

    If you have a story idea, a correction, or simply want to say hello, we'd love to hear from you. Visit our contact page to get in touch.
